### BCGT-000062: Searcher-oracle mechanisms for large transactions
- **Subfield:** Transaction-fee incentive compatibility and collusion
- **Open:** Obtain truthful, false-name-resistant welfare guarantees when MEV-informed transactions or bundles consume substantial fractions of block capacity.
- **Status:** Research target beyond SAKA’s approximately half-optimal small-transaction guarantee and matching deterministic limitation.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Large indivisible bundles violate small-item assumptions.
- **Key works:** Bahrani, Garimidi and Roughgarden (2024), *Transaction Fee Mechanism Design in a Post-MEV World*, DOI:10.4230/LIPIcs.AFT.2024.29.
- **Related:** Budget-feasible procurement with large projects.
